Before you start

You'll answer a few guided questions about your dispute. Then, before we organise anything, we'll ask what the other side might say — this helps you prepare a balanced claim, not just a one-sided one.

  • 1 We'll walk through your situation step by step, not as an open chat.
  • 2 Any factual information about SCT process is drawn from judiciary.gov.sg and marked with a source tag — not generated freely.
  • 3 We'll deliberately ask you to consider the other party's side before finalising anything.
  • 4 At the end, you'll get an organised summary and a checklist — not a final legal document.
